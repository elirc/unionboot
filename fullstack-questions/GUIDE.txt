FULL-STACK WEB DEVELOPMENT QUESTION BANK
=======================================

A local self-study Q&A companion to the runnable unionboot exercises.
Planned inventory: 520 questions with answers, FS001 through FS520,
organized into 52 sections of 10 questions. Run the validator to confirm
that all content files are present and structurally complete.

FILES
-----
frontend-web.txt       FS001-FS260: frontend and browser foundations
backend-delivery.txt   FS261-FS520: backend and delivery engineering
frontend-web.json      Structured frontend question/answer edition
backend-delivery.json  Structured backend question/answer edition
verify.cjs             Structural integrity checks for both editions

Each JSON record has id, section, question, answer, and level.
The TXT editions contain the same questions and reference answers.
These are study questions, not executable programming exercises.
They are not included in the root runnable-exercise count.

STUDY ROUTINE
-------------
1. Choose one section (10 questions).
2. Read a question without looking at its reference answer.
3. Answer aloud or in your own separate notes. For scenarios, state your
   assumptions, explain the mechanism, and discuss one tradeoff.
4. Compare with the reference answer. More than one design may be valid;
   judge the reasoning and constraints, not exact wording.
5. Turn one scenario into a small local implementation or debugging task.
6. Revisit missed questions after two days and again after a week.

SELF-ASSESSMENT
---------------
0: Cannot explain the concept yet.
1: Recognizes the concept but misses the mechanism or key constraint.
2: Explains the mechanism correctly with a concrete example.
3: Also explains tradeoffs, failure cases, and how to test the approach.

Suggested session: five questions from each half for a balanced review.
Keep learner notes in a separate file so the reference editions remain
usable as an answer key. Do not paste real secrets or customer records
into study examples.

CHECKING THE BANK
-----------------
From C:\Users\Owner\Desktop\unionboot run:

  node fullstack-questions/verify.cjs

Expected completed inventory: 520 questions, 52 sections, FS001-FS520.
Exit 0: all structural checks passed.
Exit 1: missing content or a structural/content-consistency check failed.
Exit 2: unsupported command-line arguments.

Checks include consecutive IDs, section counts, populated answers,
normalized exact duplicate questions, and agreement between JSON and TXT.
They do NOT prove every answer factually correct, detect all conceptual
duplicates, grade your own answers, or verify application behavior.
Check framework/version-specific details against official documentation
when applying an answer to a real project.
