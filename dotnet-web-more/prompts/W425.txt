Scenario 5: Drain channel batches of 5
Semantic key: drain-channel-batches-of-5

Drain at most 5 immediately available items in FIFO order. Never block waiting for future items; leave excess queued. Return count. Observe cancellation before consuming any item. Caller owns completion.
