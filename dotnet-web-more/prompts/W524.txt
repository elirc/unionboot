W524 | Header propagation policies
Scenario 4: Allowlist X-Locale and baggage
Semantic key: allowlist-x-locale-and-baggage

Copy only X-Locale and baggage to a fresh header dictionary. Preserve all values and case-insensitive lookup. Do not forward Authorization/Cookie/Connection or other unlisted headers; do not mutate input.

Signature: public static IHeaderDictionary Propagate(IHeaderDictionary incoming)
