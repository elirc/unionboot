W526 | Header propagation policies
Scenario 6: Allowlist X-Client-Version and baggage
Semantic key: allowlist-x-client-version-and-baggage

Copy only X-Client-Version and baggage to a fresh header dictionary. Preserve all values and case-insensitive lookup. Do not forward Authorization/Cookie/Connection or other unlisted headers; do not mutate input.

Signature: public static IHeaderDictionary Propagate(IHeaderDictionary incoming)
