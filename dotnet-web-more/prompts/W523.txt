W523 | Header propagation policies
Scenario 3: Allowlist X-Tenant-Id and baggage
Semantic key: allowlist-x-tenant-id-and-baggage

Copy only X-Tenant-Id and baggage to a fresh header dictionary. Preserve all values and case-insensitive lookup. Do not forward Authorization/Cookie/Connection or other unlisted headers; do not mutate input.

Signature: public static IHeaderDictionary Propagate(IHeaderDictionary incoming)
