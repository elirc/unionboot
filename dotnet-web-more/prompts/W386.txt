Scenario 6: HMAC-SHA256 hex verify for echo-key
Semantic key: hmac-sha256-hex-verify-for-echo-key

Verify an HMAC-SHA256 webhook signature supplied as lowercase hex. Malformed hex (odd length or non-hex) ->400. Constant-time compare against HMACSHA256.HashData over the UTF-8 secret and payload: match ->200 {verified:true}, mismatch ->401.

Signature: public static IResult Verify(string payload,string signatureHex,string secret)
