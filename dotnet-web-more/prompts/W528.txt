W528 | Header propagation policies
Scenario 8: Allowlist X-Request-Id and baggage
Semantic key: allowlist-x-request-id-and-baggage

Copy only X-Request-Id and baggage to a fresh header dictionary. Preserve all values and case-insensitive lookup. Do not forward Authorization/Cookie/Connection or other unlisted headers; do not mutate input.

Signature: public static IHeaderDictionary Propagate(IHeaderDictionary incoming)
