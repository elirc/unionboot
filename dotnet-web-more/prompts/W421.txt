Scenario 1: Drain channel batches of 1
Semantic key: drain-channel-batches-of-1

Drain at most 1 immediately available items in FIFO order. Never block waiting for future items; leave excess queued. Return count. Observe cancellation before consuming any item. Caller owns completion.
